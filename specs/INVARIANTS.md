# Invariants

These properties must remain true across experiments.

## INV-001 Human creative authority

No automated score, model judgment, or external tool may promote a candidate to featured/public status without explicit human owner selection.

Validation: gallery state and final integration history.

## INV-002 Primary-source product truth

Strong claims about what a subject product does, has shipped, or has observed must resolve to its own authoritative repository evidence or an explicitly identified public source.

Validation: source receipts in the experiment brief / candidate links.

## INV-003 External research is not product proof

A paper, market survey, benchmark, or third-party statistic may establish that a market problem exists. It may not be presented as proof that the subject product solves that problem unless the product itself measured that outcome.

Validation: evidence review.

## INV-004 Human recognition before machinery

The primary marketing candidate must give the target audience a recognizable problem, moment, question, or consequence before requiring them to understand internal architecture or methodology.

This is an editorial invariant reviewed by the human owner, not a mechanically provable truth.

## INV-005 No generic template authority

Fluffy System may define narrative functions and quality gates, but must not require a fixed section order, design component library, palette, or page template across products.

Validation: candidate diversity review.

## INV-006 Honest OSS attribution

A candidate may be labelled as produced/edited by an OSS tool only if that tool was actually executed on the artifact. Otherwise use language such as `workflow-informed`, `reference-inspired`, or `native implementation`.

Validation: adapter record.

## INV-007 Native HTML control

Every experiment subject receives at least one self-contained HTML/CSS/JS control candidate so external tooling is compared against a known working baseline rather than assumed superior.

Validation: gallery inventory.

## INV-008 3D has a job

A Three.js/WebGL candidate must use depth, transformation, spatial relationships, simulation, or interaction to express the product thesis. Decorative particle fields alone do not satisfy the 3D variant requirement.

Validation: design review.

## INV-009 3D fails soft

Core copy, navigation, evidence links, and CTAs remain usable if WebGL is unavailable, reduced motion is requested, or the external Three.js module fails to load.

Validation: fallback inspection.

## INV-010 Hidden gallery boundary

Experiment candidates must not be added to the main homepage/project navigation, sitemap, or featured-project list until explicitly selected.

The gallery may be directly reachable by URL.

Validation: Design Bakery route and project inventory review.

## INV-011 Public marketing avoids em dash characters

Generated public-facing marketing copy should not use the em dash character unless the owner explicitly changes this rule.

Validation: text review/search.

## INV-012 Provenance should not dominate the pitch

Evidence receipts must remain available, but ordinary candidate pages should not lead with evidence-class metadata, schema status, compliance vocabulary, or audit-style control panels unless that is itself the market audience and chosen story.

Validation: editorial review.
